YANGHOMESTAY - BO ANH THAT DEMO
================================

Muc dich:
- Bo nay gom mapping anh that phu hop voi cac can YangHomestay hien tai.
- Anh duoc chon tu cac trang Pexels co ghi "Free to use".
- Script se tai anh TRUC TIEP tu Pexels ve may cua ban, khong hotlink trong website.

CACH DUNG NHANH NHAT
1. Giai nen file ZIP.
2. Double-click: TAI_ANH_YANGHOMESTAY.bat
3. Cho script chay xong. Anh JPG se tu dong xuat hien trong cac folder.
4. Copy cac folder anh vao:
   WEBODER/assets/images/homestays/
5. Dua file MAPPING_FOR_FREEBUFF.md cho Freebuff de no gan anh vao code/Supabase.

NGUYEN TAC MAPPING
- Cầu Giấy Business: workspace hien dai.
- Tây Hồ: cua kinh lon, view nuoc/sunset, workcation.
- Trúc Bạch: lake-view / balcony chi dung khi unit co tag/tien nghi tuong ung.
- Phố Cổ: vintage, go, gach, warm lighting.
- Gia dinh: living room + bep + khong gian rong.
- Bathtub: chi dung anh bathtub cho unit co amenity bathtub.

Luu y:
- Day la anh DEMO/stock, khong phai anh chup thuc te cua bat dong san YangHomestay.
- Truoc khi kinh doanh that, thay bang anh chup thuc te cua tung can.
- File SOURCES.csv luu nguon tung anh de doi chieu.
